# Tone Lab runtime analysis

Tone Lab loads the Mandarin audio index and dictionary readings from wenbun-assets. It does not load or persist precomputed scores. Selected audio is decoded to mono in the browser, then sent to a Web Worker for pitch extraction, contour/movement scoring, waveform envelopes, and a Hann-windowed FFT spectrogram. Recordings use the same path. A bounded memory cache retains up to twelve downloaded audio blobs and their analyses; leaving the page releases them.

Contour fit compares 13 samples of a syllable's cleaned semitone contour with tone templates, with a fitted baseline and bounded scale. Pitch movement measures rise/fall, range, and dipping features. Candidate scores combine 47% contour fit and 53% pitch movement. Tone agreement counts matching top guesses among non-neutral syllables with sufficient usable pitch. These heuristics are experimental and not independently calibrated pronunciation grades.

Overlay shows raw dots and a smoothed clean contour. Raw preserves individual estimates. Clean removes weak estimates, corrects isolated octave errors, and smooths contiguous voiced runs in log-frequency space. A monotone cubic curve connects clean points without crossing unvoiced gaps. Relative uses semitones around the median pitch. The waveform uses min/max amplitude envelopes; the spectrogram uses a 512-sample Hann window at 8 kHz, displaying 0–4 kHz and −85 to 0 dBFS.

Listening and Explore share a background queue of five preloaded audio clips and their runtime analyses, whether or not the clear-only filter is on. Next clip and Random clip take an accepted entry immediately when available and refills the queue in the background. The first entry can be used before all five are ready. Independent workers isolate background analysis from foreground work; changing the filter resets the queue; entering Speaking or leaving cancels preloading. Each refill checks up to 80 shuffled candidates, stopping early after three consecutive download failures to avoid an unbounded offline retry loop. Clear-only is enabled by default, with an adjustable 50/100 minimum combined candidate score per syllable. Matching weak fits can pass this floor; the “Weak fit” label itself is not a veto. Every syllable must also have at least 45% clean coverage, a top-two margin of at least 12, and no other diagnostic flags. Changing the threshold clears eligibility decisions and the ready queue, but cached audio and evidence are reused. The filter requires tone agreement, and rejects missing/ambiguous keys, neutral tones, and phrases with unresolved 一/不 sandhi. A failed search can be retried or the filter disabled. The annotated spoken key determines listening results. Third-tone sandhi is applied; neutral is represented as 0.

Tests: `npm test -- src/lib/toneLab/`. No reference score is used. No audio or scores are uploaded or written to the assets repository.

The Minimum score control is collapsed by default under a gray, underlined Advanced settings control next to the clarity information icon. Both the control and settings are hidden when clear-only is off. Known bad recordings are excluded from every Tone Lab source list and load path via `src/lib/toneLab/avoidList.ts`, regardless of the clarity filter. The temporary avoid list currently excludes `hugolpz-audio-cmn-64k/syllabs/cmn-ri3.mp3`.
